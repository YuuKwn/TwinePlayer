Input Lab

Diagnostics are controlled by the app toolbar.

Manual checklist

  1. Try both Compact and Comfortable profiles, then tap/click the app chrome and this story.
  2. Test one and two-finger touch, cancellation (including leaving the surface), mouse primary/secondary click and wheel.
  3. Activate links/choices, form controls, the image preview/context path, and the dynamic add/remove controls.
  4. Scroll this page, edit the contenteditable region, drag the exclusion card, and interact with canvas/SVG.
  5. Open app fullscreen and the diagnostics report; confirm focus returns to this story after closing overlays.
